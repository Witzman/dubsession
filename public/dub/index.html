A generative dub techno engine, built in the browser. Early.

p01 — Basic · 125 BPM, G minor, the centre of the round: four on the floor, the chord on every offbeat eighth, one rimshot a bar, drenched. The first attempt, kept for comparison and level-matched to p02 so the louder one is not simply the better one.
